Your job search, step by step

Job Radar collects postings, helps you decide which fit, and prepares applications for your review. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

1

Find jobs

Job sources are the websites and searches Job Radar checks. The Employers directory also includes companies that are not being checked yet.

2

Review matches

Your selected local model reads each posting and gives it a fit score. Set the strong-match threshold in Settings → Automatic draft preparation. Reviews run in the background; the Queue shows progress.

3

Decide what to keep

Open Jobs, read a posting, then choose Shortlist, Later, or Ignore. The score is a guide; you make the decision.

4

Review an application

Codex can prepare a CV and message for a shortlisted job. Check the draft in Applications. For a LinkedIn posting, use Check LinkedIn Apply to find its application method. For Easy Apply, answer the questions, save, then inspect the remaining steps. Review every answer and the final form before approving a supported submission. Nothing is sent without your approval.

Source

An application package waiting for your review.
